03

THE EVERYDAY FRICTION

The problem

One home. Too many things to remember.

Managing a home means juggling utility bills, meter readings, property taxes, insurance renewals, technical inspections, maintenance, and important documents.

Bills in your inbox Documents in a drawer Dates in your head Maintenance on a sticky note
The problem isn’t a lack of apps. It’s that managing a single home requires too many disconnected tools and too much manual effort.
04

THE VALUE PROPOSITION

Our solution

Everything related to your home. Finally connected.

FirstFlat aims to centralize home management in one intelligent platform, turning scattered information into clear next steps.

Smart Reminders

Bills, taxes, meter readings, inspections, and insurance renewals in one personalized calendar.

AI Document Assistant

Proposed AI extraction of deadlines, obligations, and relevant details, with user confirmation.

Home Dashboard

A clear view of expenses, maintenance, equipment, and the property’s history.

Digital Home Passport

A lasting, organized digital record of the property and its important documents.

FUTURE INTEGRATIONS

Automated workflows with utility providers, insurers, and maintenance services are part of the long-term vision.

We don’t just want to store information.
We want to help users turn it into action.

THE EXISTING LANDSCAPE

Competition

Existing solutions address parts of home and property management, creating an opportunity to explore a more unified, consumer-friendly experience.

DIRECT COMPETITORS

Property management platforms

Rentzz TenantCloud Buildium

Established tools in the rental and property management space.

INDIRECT COMPETITORS

The everyday workaround

  • Google Calendar and Excel
  • Banking and utility provider apps
  • Document storage platforms

Useful individually; often managed separately.

Bills, meters, insurance, inspections, and notifications already exist in competing products. Our opportunity must be validated through the overall experience.

07

OUR PROPOSED DIFFERENTIATION

Competitive advantage

From property management software to a personal operating system for every home.
  • 01

    Built for everyday homeowners

    A home-first experience for owners and tenants, alongside professional property management.

  • 02

    Less manual setup

    AI-assisted onboarding that aims to reduce repetitive data entry.

  • 03

    Information that prompts action

    Proactive reminders and proposed task creation from confirmed document details.

  • 04

    One connected ecosystem

    A shared platform for individuals and businesses, with potential integrations and service partnerships.

The proposed advantage is the combination of accessibility, automation, and complete home lifecycle management, rather than any single feature.

08

HOW WE’LL LEARN

Key metrics

Measure real usefulness. Then sustainable growth.

01 / ADOPTION

Monthly Active Homes

Properties actively managed through FirstFlat each month.

02 / LONG-TERM VALUE

User Retention

Users who return over time, including after 90 days.

03 / REAL ACTION

Task Completion Rate

The share of reminders that result in completed actions.

04 / MONETIZATION

Free-to-Paid Conversion

The percentage of free users upgrading to premium.

05 / RECURRING GROWTH

MRR

Monthly Recurring Revenue from paid subscriptions.

06 / UNIT ECONOMICS

CAC vs. LTV

Customer acquisition cost compared with lifetime value.

These are planned measurements, not reported results. Interviews and pilots will help define targets and test retention.

WHAT IT TAKES TO BUILD

Cost structure

Building a dependable home assistant means investing in the product, the infrastructure, and the people using it.

  • 01

    Product & engineering

    Software development and AI integration.

  • 02

    Infrastructure & storage

    Cloud hosting, databases, and document storage.

  • 03

    AI & third-party services

    AI processing and external APIs.

  • 04

    Distribution & growth

    Marketing and customer acquisition.

  • 05

    Trust & support

    Security, compliance, and customer support.

The business opportunity: a software-based model with potentially low marginal costs per additional property, provided AI usage and support costs remain controlled.
