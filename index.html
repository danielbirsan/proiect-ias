THE FIKI PROJECT, A LITTLE DEEPER

Big idea. Grounded thinking.

What makes FirstFlat different?+

Calendars, spreadsheets, storage apps, and rental platforms already cover parts of the problem. Rentzz already offers bills, meters, insurance, inspections, notifications, and some automation.

Our proposed distinction is the complete home experience for owner-occupiers and tenants, with AI-assisted onboarding and a personal home calendar. These are ideas to build and validate, rather than established competitive advantages.

How could the business grow?+

A freemium product for individuals, paid family plans, and recurring B2B subscriptions form the proposed model. Potential distribution partners include insurers, property developers, authorized maintenance providers, and homeowners’ associations.

A developer could hand over a home’s documents and warranties in FirstFlat. Future service bookings could add transaction revenue. Partnerships and integrations still need to be developed.

What would success look like?+

We would track completed home setups, reminders enabled, monthly usage, 90-day retention, and willingness to pay. For businesses, time saved matters as much as portfolio size.

Annual reminders alone may not sustain usage. Monthly bills and readings, periodic renewals, and occasional repairs must create lasting value. Key costs include development, hosting, storage, AI processing, security, support, and distribution.

How would reminders and AI stay useful?+

Dates should come from the user’s documents, provider settings, and applicable rules. Extracted information and uncertain dates must be confirmed by the user. This demo uses illustrative examples, not live AI or connected accounts.

Heating system inspections, gas installation checks, and gas installation revisions are distinct activities. See the Romanian regulations; there is no single universal home maintenance calendar.
