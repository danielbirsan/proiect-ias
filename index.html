Problem

Students struggle to keep up with large volumes of learning material and often lose focus when planning study sessions.

Solution / Value Proposition

SmartStudy helps students build personalized study plans, track progress, and receive adaptive recommendations that improve learning outcomes.

Customer Segments

  • High school students preparing for exams
  • University students managing multiple courses
  • Lifelong learners pursuing certifications

Competition

  • General productivity apps (Notion, Trello)
  • Learning apps with static plans
  • Traditional tutoring schedules

Our Advantage

  • AI-guided adaptive plans based on actual study behavior
  • Simple dashboard focused on education metrics
  • Affordable student-first pricing

Key Metrics

  • Weekly active users
  • Average completed study sessions per user
  • Retention after 30 days

Cost Structure

  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting
  • Product development and maintenance
  • Marketing and partnerships

Revenue Streams

  • Monthly premium subscriptions
  • Institutional plans for schools and universities
  • Optional add-ons for advanced analytics
